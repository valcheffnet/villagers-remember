# Tiers, errands and rewards

How standing, errands and rewards work, with the numbers the mod uses. Most of them can be changed in
`config/villagersremember.json` or in the settings screen. Code comments point to the sections below.

## 1. Standing

Every errand gives standing points to the villager who asked and to their village.

| Tier | Villager | Points | Village | Points | Residents needed |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Stranger | 0 | Stranger | 0 | 0 |
| 2 | Acquaintance | 20 | Known | 60 | 2 |
| 3 | Friend | 60 | Friend | 180 | 3 |
| 4 | Good friend | 140 | Respected | 420 | 4 |
| 5 | Best friend | 280 | Hero of the Village | 840 | 5 |

A village tier needs the points and that many working residents at the same tier or higher, so one best friend is
not enough to be a Hero.

Points per errand by pack: 2-3, 3-4, 4-5, 5-6, 6-8.

**Trials of trust.** When a player reaches the points for the next tier with a villager, that villager first asks
for a trial: an errand 1.5 times larger that pays double. The first trial of each tier with a profession also gives
an item from that profession. The very first trial a player ever gets asks them to have an ender chest (kept, not
taken), because the treasury pays into it.

**Losing standing.** Hitting a villager costs 2 points with them and the village, killing one costs 25 with the
village. Standing below zero raises prices (section 5).

## 2. Who offers errands

- A villager offers errands only from the pack of their tier with the player.
- Errands a day by trade level: Novice 1, Apprentice 2, Journeyman 2, Expert 3, Master 3. A Respected village adds
  one more.
- A player carries up to 5 errands in the journal.
- Nitwits offer small, silly errands (1-4 minutes).

### 2.1 Gradual trust

In a village only a circle of villagers offers a player errands. Every player has their own circle.

- The village picks the first villager, the one who dares to talk to a stranger. Others point to them.
- Places in the circle: 1, plus the tier reached with each member, plus the village tier (Known +1, Friend +3,
  Respected +6, Hero: everyone), plus one for every village order the player helped to finish.
- A villager the player trades up a level joins on top of that. So does one who remembers the player through
  vanilla gossip: cured by them, or traded with a lot lately (trading gossip 20 or more).
- A member who dies leaves the place empty. Only when nobody in the circle is left does someone new dare, the next
  game day. A member turned into a zombie villager comes back when cured.
- Villagers outside the circle still trade.

## 3. Rewards

Errands pay experience and standing. Emeralds are off by default (`/errands emeralds on|off`); experience can be
scaled with `/errands xp <multiplier>`.

- The pay comes from the minutes of work (section 4) times a rate per pack: 0.6, 0.8, 1.0, 1.3, 1.6 emeralds a
  minute, at most 64 per errand. With emeralds off it is paid as experience.
- An errand always pays at least 1.5 times what its goods would sell for to a villager.
- Village standing adds to the pay: Known +5%, Friend +10%, Respected +15%, Hero +20%.
- Items come once: from trials (section 1), the top reward of each profession (section 4) and one reforge of each
  piece at the smiths.

## 4. Packs and effort

Each profession has five packs, one per tier, generated by `tools/gen_errands.py`. A pack's errands are sized in
minutes of work for one daily errand:

| Pack | T1 | T2 | T3 | T4 | T5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Minutes | 4-8 | 8-12 | 12-18 | 18-25 | 25-35 |

- T1 assumes no farms: crops from village fields, wild sugar cane, animals hunted or bred with food picked by hand.
  Each pack after it expects a bit more of a farm, and T5 the usual farms and a trading hall.
- From T3 on there are orders for several goods at once.
- Harvest and mining count what the blocks gave: "harvest and bring 18 carrots" is done with 18 carrots, and
  Fortune helps with ores.
- **Top rewards.** Each profession has a top reward with a few options to choose from, given once.

### 4a. Kinds of errands

21 kinds, alone or paired with bringing the result: bring, craft, smelt or cook, make at a station, harvest, mine,
plant, use bone meal, breed, tame, shear, milk, catch fish, defeat mobs, fill a bucket, buy from a trader, enchant,
brew, cure zombie villagers, build a golem, and visit a structure or biome (with a map to it).

## 5. Prices

Prices follow standing, inside the vanilla trading screen.

- Discount: 4% per villager tier and 6% per village tier above the first, at most 40%.
- The patron of a village trades there at 45% off.
- Below zero standing, prices rise 1% per point, at most 50%.
- Vanilla's own changes (demand, Hero of the Village effect, cured villagers) stay on top.

## 6. Treasury

Villagers who trust a player set part of their work aside for them. The emeralds go to the player's ender chest
on request.

- Each working resident gives a share a game day by the player's tier with them: 0, 0.0104, 0.0208, 0.0333 or 0.05
  emeralds. Later residents count less.
- The village tier multiplies it: 0.5, 0.75, 1.0, 1.25, 1.5.
- Every other friendly village adds 5%, up to 25%.
- A village keeps at most 504 game days of pay (a real week of play).
- A Hero village of best friends gives about 0.6 emeralds a game day.

The treasury pays whether errand emeralds are on or off.

## 7. End game

- **Fame.** Points from errands, trials, contracts, village orders, villages found and advancements. Titles in chat
  and TAB: Helper (100), Benefactor (500), Legend (1500).
- **Contracts.** Once a game week every best friend offers a contract: three hard parts, about 90 minutes in all,
  paying 1.2 times the usual rate, at most 160 emeralds.
- **Legendary items.** The third contract for a profession brings its legendary item, named after the villager
  who gave it ("Stoyan's Hoe"), with every fitting enchantment at its best.
- **Village order.** Once a game month (30 game days) a village asks the whole server for one big order, sized by
  its regular players (those who played on at least 3 game days of the last month). Anyone brings what they have.
  When it is done every helper with at least 2% of it is paid by share: 80 emeralds per regular player split
  between them, plus experience, fame and standing. An order left unfinished pays half.
- **Patron.** A player who is Hero of the Village and has passed every trial with at least five of its villagers
  qualifies. The one with the most points there is the patron: their head and name at the bell, the patron
  discount, and the right to name the village.

### 7.5 Advancements

The mod has its own advancement tab for errands, trust, villages, exploration, the treasury, contracts and fame.
Players who did the work before the tab existed get the advancements when they join.
